Product Case Study

Make-Ready Intelligence Platform (DMRB)

Designed & built

AI operations platform for apartment unit turns, proven in a live 2,444-unit residential operation: 15 daily users across 5 roles, 8+ months in production.

  • Found the problem from 10+ years inside operations: unit-readiness knowledge was scattered across spreadsheets, verbal updates and property-system exports, with no audit trail and no gate against premature move-ins.
  • Designed a 57-table PostgreSQL data model around the unit lifecycle, with append-only event history, field-level import audit trails and scoped vendor access by token.
  • Turned operational judgment into a deterministic rules engine: 11 breach signals (move-in risk, SLA breach, inspection breach, stale units) evaluated in strict priority order and feeding 22 ranked action queues. It flags move-in risk up to 3 business days early.
  • Deployed a GPT-4o assistant grounded in live data through one snapshot pipeline that the UI, the operations views and the AI all read. The AI recommends and never writes to business state.
  • Built anti-hallucination guardrails: intent classification, role-scoped context, server-computed summaries, token budgets, rate limiting, and a deterministic fallback when the model is unavailable.
  • Drove adoption across leasing, management, field and vendor users through hands-on training. The platform became the daily system of record for scheduling, vendor dispatch and SLA tracking, and supported a seven-business-day turn standard.
  • Scale: four production services, 950 commits across backend and frontend, 358 test files. Now leading a multi-tenant Python/FastAPI rewrite with tenant isolation, Alembic migrations and 594 tests wired into CI.
